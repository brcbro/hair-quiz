Wash day steps + daily morning refresh

Your Wash Day Routine

Every 3 days · About 20 minutes

›
Step 1

Shampoo

  • Apply shampoo to your scalp before lathering, oil lives beneath your hair and shampoo needs to reach it as a liquid before it foams
  • Oil is difficult to remove. Use firm pressure from your fingertips to lift the layers off your scalp
  • Shampoo goes on your scalp only. Your mids and ends do not produce oil so shampoo will dry them out. Use only the suds to keep them clean
  • Wash twice every time. The first wash breaks down oil and buildup but doesn't remove it completely. The second wash finishes the job
Step 2

Conditioner

  • Apply conditioner to your mids and ends only, never your roots
  • Applying it to your roots will cause oiliness and force an early wash
  • Use enough to get a slippery feeling through your entire ponytail. Any area that doesn't feel slippery isn't getting conditioned
  • Let it sit for 2 minutes before rinsing
Step 3

Leave-in Conditioner

  • Towel dry your hair first. Too much water will block the product from absorbing
  • Hold the bottle at least 12 inches away from your hair to get an even, fine mist
  • Spray roots to tips covering all of your hair
  • Fine hair needs 8 to 12 sprays. Use enough to feel light coverage throughout
Step 4

Detangle

  • Always start from the ends and work your way up
  • Starting from the top brings tangles down on top of each other which slows you down and causes breakage
  • Always use a brush with flexible bristles. Combs cause breakage when they catch
Step 4

Blow Dry Cream

  • Spread a pea sized amount across your palms for shoulder length hair
  • Longer or thicker hair can use more
  • Work it into your ends first, they need the most love. Then slowly work your way up so your mids get a bit less and your roots get the least
Step 5

Volume Blow Dry Mist

  • Apply to the root area on the crown of your head only
  • Part your hair and spray down towards the roots. Try to avoid getting it on your mids
  • Use circular motions with your fingertips to spread it evenly throughout your crown
Step 5

Rough Dry

  • Focus the blow dryer on the underneath layers of your hair. The top layers will dry on their own during the blow dry
  • Frizz is your signal to stop. The moment it appears put the dryer down and move on to the next step. How long this takes is different for everyone
Step 6

Detangle

  • Always start from the ends and work your way up
  • Starting from the top brings tangles down on top of each other which slows you down and causes breakage
  • Always use a brush with flexible bristles. Combs cause breakage when they catch
Step 7

Blow Dry

  • Always pull your hair straight with tension while drying. Loose hair dries frizzy. Tension is what creates a smooth result
  • Dry each section until it feels like a blanket fresh out of the dryer. Warm but not hot. That is your signal to move on. Continuing past that point causes heat damage
⚠️ Step 5

Pre-Dry or Air Dry

  • We strongly recommend blow drying your hair straight before ironing. Hair that air dries sets in a frizzy shape and requires much more heat to override. A quick blow dry first means less heat, less work, and a style that actually holds
Step 6

Thermal Protectant

  • Hold the bottle at least 12 inches away from your hair so it spreads into a fine mist
  • Make sure to spray throughout your hair, not just the outside layers
  • Brush through immediately after applying. If you skip this your hair will feel crunchy
Step 7

Flat Iron, Curling Iron or Wand

  • Work in smaller sections. Large sections take so long for heat to penetrate that the outside burns while the inside is still warming up
  • Section shape matters. Flat iron sections should be wide and thin. Curling iron or wand sections should be narrow and tall
Step 4

Hair Oil

  • Never apply oil to your roots. It will cause greasiness and force an early wash
  • Less is more. Start with just a few drops and add more only if you need it
  • Apply to your ends first, they need it most, then work your way up to your mids with whatever is left

Weekly Repair Protocol

Once a week · Replaces conditioner

›
Step 1

Shampoo

  • Shampoo your hair normally — the same way you always would
  • Do not condition after. The repair mask replaces your conditioner on this wash
Step 2

Acidic Bonding Concentrate Mask

  • Apply to all damaged areas of your hair — mids and ends
  • Let it sit for 5 minutes, then rinse thoroughly
  • This rebuilds the bonds inside your hair shaft that heat and chemical damage break down
Step 3

Moroccanoil Mask

  • Apply to your mids and ends immediately after rinsing the repair mask
  • Let it sit for 8 minutes, then rinse thoroughly
  • Pairing both masks in the same wash gives faster results than either one alone

Your Morning Refresh

Every morning · Done in 5 minutes

›
Step 1

Dry Shampoo

  • Check your roots for oil. If they feel greasy, apply dry shampoo by working in sections across your entire head
  • Part hair to expose the roots and spray directly on your scalp. Never spray on mids or ends
Step 2

Detangle

  • Use an ionic brush with flexible bristles. These do more than remove tangles — they fight frizz and add shine at the same time
Step 3

Hair Oil

  • Rub a few drops between your palms and apply ends first, working up to your mids
  • Never apply to your roots
